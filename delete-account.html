Delete Your Rescue Radar Account

Users can permanently delete their Rescue Radar account directly from within the app.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Rescue Radar app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

What Happens When Your Account is Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account and profile
  • Your vehicles
  • Your job history
  • Your messages
  • Your ratings and reviews

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Data Retention

Unless required by law, Rescue Radar permanently deletes your account data after the deletion process is completed.

Any information that must be retained for legal, fraud prevention, tax, or regulatory purposes will only be retained for the minimum period required by applicable law.
