Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Horizon Performance Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Rescue Radar mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data (when using our services)
  • Vehicle information
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)
  • Communications within the app

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Connect customers with recovery operators
  • Process payments
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraud

3. Location Data

Our app requires location access to:

  • Show your location to recovery operators
  • Enable real-time tracking during assistance
  • Find nearby available operators

You can disable location access in your device settings, but this will limit app functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We share your information only:

  • With recovery operators when you request assistance
  • With payment processors (Stripe) for transactions
  • When required by law

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information, including encryption and secure servers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
